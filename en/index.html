TickerWall Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

TickerWall is a read-only Apple TV app for viewing public cryptocurrency market information. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data We Collect

TickerWall does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising identifier.

Tracking

TickerWall does not track you across apps or websites and does not use tracking SDKs or targeted advertising.

Market Data

TickerWall requests public cryptocurrency market data from Binance public endpoints, including HTTPS REST and secure WebSocket connections. These requests are used only to display market prices, charts, order book depth, and recent trades in the app. Binance or network providers may receive standard technical request information such as IP address and request metadata according to their own policies.

Local Preferences

TickerWall may store display preferences, such as the red/green rise and fall color setting, locally on your Apple TV using system storage. These preferences stay on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Financial Disclaimer

TickerWall provides market information only. It does not provide financial advice, trading, brokerage, custody, wallet, account, or payment services.

Children

TickerWall is not intended to collect data from children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version will be available at the privacy policy URL provided in App Store Connect.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the GitHub repository: https://github.com/17classdeveloper-design/TickerWall
