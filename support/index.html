TickerWall Support

TickerWall is a read-only Apple TV app for viewing public cryptocurrency market information.

Contact

For support questions, bug reports, or review inquiries, open an issue in the GitHub repository:

https://github.com/17classdeveloper-design/TickerWall/issues

Common Notes

  • No account or login is required.
  • The app does not provide trading, brokerage, custody, wallet, account, or payment services.
  • Market data is loaded from public Binance endpoints and may be unavailable if the network or data provider is unavailable.
  • The app provides market information only and does not provide financial advice.
